Your Content Stays On Your Device

Aura Affirmations has no accounts, no login, and no servers of Our own. Everything You create in the app is stored locally on Your Device and is never uploaded to Us. This includes:

  • Your saved and favorited affirmations
  • Your daily, monthly, and yearly goals, and their completion status
  • Your journal reflections, moods, and any photos You attach to them
  • Any photos You choose as affirmation backgrounds
  • Your text, font, and color preferences

We cannot read this information, and it is not backed up to Us. Deleting the app removes this data from Your Device.

Photos and Camera

With Your prior permission, the Application can access Your Device's photo library and camera so You can set a photo as an affirmation background or attach a photo to a journal reflection. These photos are used only inside the app on Your Device. They are not uploaded to Us, not analyzed, and not shared with any third party.

No face data or biometric data is collected, stored, or shared. You may decline or later revoke these permissions in Your Device's Settings; the rest of the app continues to work normally.

Sharing

When You choose to share an affirmation, the app creates an image on Your Device and hands it to Your Device's own share sheet. You decide where it goes. We do not receive a copy.

This data is collected by Google as a Service Provider, not by Us, and is governed by Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Advertising is never linked to the contents of Your affirmations, goals, journal entries, or photos.

App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, the Application asks for Your permission before Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for tracking. If You decline, You will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on Your Device.

You can review or change Your choice at any time from the settings panel inside the app, under Ad Privacy Settings.
