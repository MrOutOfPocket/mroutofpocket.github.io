IDENTIFY ME TAG PRIVACY POLICY Effective Date: February 28, 2026 Identify Me Tag ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Identify Me Tag handles information when you use the mobile app. 1. Information You Provide Identify Me Tag lets you create a personal tag with details such as your display name, handle, pronouns, gender, tagline, link, phone number, email, bio, emoji, vibe, and profile/background photos. 2. Permissions Identify Me Tag may request access to: - Camera: to take profile or background photos. - Photo Library: to choose existing photos. You can deny or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings. 3. How Your Data Is Stored Your tag draft is stored locally on your device so your setup is saved between sessions. 4. How We Use Information Information is used only to: - Build and display your tag. - Save your draft locally. - Let you share your tag text or image using your device's native sharing options. 5. Data Sharing We do not sell your personal information. We do not run ads in the app. We do not require account creation to use core app features. If you choose to share content, the information you share is sent to the app/service you select through your device's share sheet. Their privacy practices apply once shared. 6. Data Retention and Deletion Because data is stored on-device, you control retention. You can clear or change your content at any time in the app. Removing the app generally removes local app data from your device (subject to device backup settings). 7. Children's Privacy Identify Me Tag is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. 8. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted with a revised Effective Date. 9. Contact For privacy questions, contact: mydevlifestyle@gmail.com
