CarDex

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of Your information when You use the Service and tells You about Your privacy rights and how the law protects You.

CarDex identifies cars from a photo, turns each one into a collectible trading card with stats and a rarity, and lets You build a personal garage of everything You spot. It has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask You for Your name, email address, phone number, or any other directly identifying information, and there is no way to give it to Us through the app.

What We Collect

Information You give Us

Almost none. During first launch You may optionally type a display name so the app can greet You by it; this is never required, is stored only on Your Device, and is never sent to Us or anyone else.

Information stored on Your Device

When You save a car, the Application stores its photo, make, model, nickname, stats, specs, the date You spotted it, and which trophies You have unlocked in a local database on Your Device. This is Your personal garage. It never leaves Your Device, is not transmitted to Us, and is deleted when You remove that car from Your garage or uninstall the Application.

Camera and Photo Library

With Your permission, the Application uses Your Device's camera to photograph a car, or Your photo library so You can identify a car from a picture You already took. These permissions are used only to obtain the single photo You choose to identify or to save a car card You choose to share; the library is never browsed or uploaded in the background.

The photo You choose to identify

When You spot a car, that one photo is sent to the AI identification service configured in the Application — Google's Gemini API, or Anthropic's Claude API — to be identified, and the result (make, model, specs, stats and a rarity) is returned to the Application. The provider processes the image only to generate that response; it is not attached to an account or profile, because the Application has none. Their handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Anthropic Privacy Policy.

If no identification service is configured, the Application instead uses a built-in offline field guide, and in that case no photo leaves Your Device at all.

So that there are no surprises, these are the only places the Application shows an ad:

Your Choices and Rights

Ad personalisation

  • iOS: the Application asks for Your permission before any tracking, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If You decline, You will still see ads, but they will be non-personalised. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where You can reset or delete Your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation.

If You are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland (GDPR)

CarDex does not yet present a dedicated EEA/UK consent dialog of its own. Until it does, Google's advertising SDK applies its own default protections for users in these regions, which may include serving only non-personalised or limited advertising. You still control tracking at all times through Your Device's own privacy settings described above. Regardless of that dialog, You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the processing of Your Personal Data, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with Your local supervisory authority.

If You are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell Personal Data for money. Sharing an advertising identifier for personalised advertising may be treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under California law. You may opt out using the Device controls listed above, which We honour.

Deleting Your data

Because We hold no Personal Data about You on Our servers, there is nothing for Us to delete on request. You can delete any saved car — and its photo — from within the Application at any time, or remove everything at once by uninstalling the Application.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13, and We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children under 13. If You are a parent or guardian and You believe Your child has provided Us with Personal Data, please contact Us and We will take steps to remove that information.

A Note on Accuracy

AI identification can be wrong, and the stats, specs and estimated values shown on a card are entertainment-grade estimates for the identified variant — not a professional appraisal or a valuation of any actual vehicle.
