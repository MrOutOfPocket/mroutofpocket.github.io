Mr Out Of Pocket Scheduler

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Mr Out Of Pocket Scheduler ("the Scheduler") is a private, self-hosted social media scheduling tool, built on the open-source Postiz software. It is operated by Mr Out Of Pocket and is used only to plan and publish posts promoting our own apps to social media accounts that we own. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside our team can sign up for it.

What Information the Scheduler Handles

When we connect one of our own social media accounts (such as TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X or Bluesky), the Scheduler receives and stores:

  • Access tokens issued by that platform, so the Scheduler can publish on our behalf.
  • Basic profile information for that account, such as the account name, username and profile picture.
  • The content we create for posting: captions, images and videos.
  • Basic performance statistics the platform returns for our own posts, such as views and likes.

The Scheduler does not collect data about other people, does not read other users' content, and does not access direct messages.

How Information Is Used

This information is used only to create, schedule and publish our own posts, and to see how those posts perform. We do not sell, rent or share it with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Where Information Is Stored

The Scheduler runs on computers we control. Tokens, content and statistics are stored in its own database there, not with a third-party scheduling service. Media files are made available over HTTPS only so that the platforms can fetch them when a post is published.

Third-Party Platforms

When a post is published, the content is sent to the platform we chose, and is then governed by that platform's own privacy policy and terms, for example TikTok, Pinterest and Google / YouTube.

Removing Access and Deleting Data

Access can be revoked at any time from the connected platform's own settings (for example, TikTok's "Manage app permissions"). Disconnecting an account in the Scheduler deletes its stored access tokens. To request deletion of any other data related to an account, email us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

The Scheduler is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective as soon as they are posted on this page.
