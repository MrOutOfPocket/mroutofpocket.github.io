What We Collect

Information You give Us

None. The Application has no registration, no login, no profile and no free-text entry.

Information stored on Your Device

The Application saves Your game progress — high score, feathers, unlocked birds, upgrades, settings and statistics — locally on Your Device. This data never leaves Your Device, is not transmitted to Us, and is deleted when You uninstall the Application. In the browser version this data is held in Your browser's local storage and is removed when You clear Your browser data.

Information collected automatically by advertising

The mobile versions of the Application (Android and iOS) display advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To serve, cap, measure and prevent fraud in those advertisements, Google's advertising SDK may collect and process:

DataWhyLinked to You?
Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA or IDFV) Selecting ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention, measurement Not linked to an identity by Us
Coarse device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language, coarse region derived from IP) Ad compatibility and relevance Not linked to an identity by Us
Ad interaction events (ad shown, ad clicked, reward earned) Measuring ad performance and paying out rewards in-game Not linked to an identity by Us

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent controller under its own policies. We receive only aggregate, non-identifying reporting (for example, "this many ads were shown"). We never receive Your advertising identifier.

Google's handling of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

The browser version

The version of Droppin' Heat playable in a web browser shows no advertisements and contains no advertising SDK. It collects nothing beyond the local save data described above.

Where Ads Appear

So that there are no surprises, these are the only two places the mobile Application shows an ad:

  • A full-screen ad after some runs. It can appear as You leave the game-over screen, never during play and never on Your first runs. There is a minimum time gap between these ads.
  • An optional ad You choose to watch. On the bird selection screen You may choose to watch a rewarded video to unlock a bird. This is entirely optional — every bird can also be unlocked by playing and earning feathers — and nothing is shown unless You tap the button.

Your Choices and Rights

Ad personalisation

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where You can reset or delete Your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The Application asks for Your permission before any tracking; if You decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised.

If You are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland (GDPR)

Before any ad is requested, the Application presents a Google-certified consent message allowing You to accept or refuse personalised advertising. You may change or withdraw that choice at any time from within the consent options presented by the Application. You also have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the processing of Your Personal Data, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with Your local supervisory authority.

If You are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell Personal Data for money. Sharing an advertising identifier for personalised advertising may be treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under California law. You may opt out using the Device controls listed above, which We honour.

Deleting Your data

Because We hold no Personal Data about You on Our servers, there is nothing for Us to delete on request. Uninstalling the Application removes all locally stored progress. To remove data held by Google, use the Device controls above or contact Google directly.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13, and We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children under 13. If You are a parent or guardian and You believe Your child has provided Us with Personal Data, please contact Us and We will take steps to remove that information.

Where the Application is distributed to a mixed or child-directed audience, advertising is configured to serve only ads appropriate for that audience and personalised advertising is disabled, in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Google Play's Families policy and Apple's Kids Category requirements.
