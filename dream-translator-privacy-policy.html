Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what Dream Translator ("the App") does and does not do with your information.

There Is No Account, and No Server We Operate

The App does not ask you to sign in. Dreams you save are stored only in the App's local storage on your own device. We do not operate a backend server or database of our own.

Dream Interpretation (Google Gemini)

When you ask the App to interpret a dream, the text you typed is sent directly to Google's Gemini AI API so it can generate the interpretation. That request is not linked to a name, account, or any other identifying information collected by the App, because the App does not collect any. The interpretation is processed by Google under its own privacy policy and terms, which we do not control:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Gemini API Additional Terms of Service

Please avoid typing sensitive personal information (such as full names, addresses, or health details) into a dream description, since that text is sent to a third-party AI service to generate your interpretation.

Advertising

The App shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. Ad requests are configured as non-personalized. Google and its advertising partners may still use device-level signals (such as approximate location from IP address and ad performance data) for basic ad delivery and fraud prevention, under their own privacy policies linked above.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective as soon as they are posted on this page.
