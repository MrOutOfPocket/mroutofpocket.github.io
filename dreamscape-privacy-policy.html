What We Collect

Information You give Us

Almost none. The dream description You type is used only to compose Your artwork, as described below, and is never sent to Us for any other purpose.

Information stored on Your Device

When You paint a dream, the Application stores the dream text, the generated title, caption, palette, style, mood, and the resulting artwork (an image file or a generative design) in a local database and file storage on Your Device. This is Your personal gallery. It never leaves Your Device, is not transmitted to Us, and is deleted when You delete that piece or uninstall the Application.

The dream text You choose to paint

When You paint a dream, the text You wrote is sent to Google's Gemini API to compose a title, caption, palette and artwork description, and — when Your key has image quota — an AI-painted image. Google processes the text only to generate that response; it is not attached to an account or profile, because the Application has none. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

If no AI key is configured, or the image model has no quota available, the Application instead composes Your artwork entirely on Your Device using a built-in generative-art engine, and in that case no text leaves Your Device at all.

Your Choices and Rights

Ad personalisation

  • iOS: the Application asks for Your permission before any tracking, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If You decline, You will still see ads, but they will be non-personalised. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where You can reset or delete Your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation.

If You are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland (GDPR)

Dreamscape does not yet present a dedicated EEA/UK consent dialog of its own. Until it does, Google's advertising SDK applies its own default protections for users in these regions, which may include serving only non-personalised or limited advertising. You still control tracking at all times through Your Device's own privacy settings described above. Regardless of that dialog, You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the processing of Your Personal Data, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with Your local supervisory authority.

If You are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell Personal Data for money. Sharing an advertising identifier for personalised advertising may be treated as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under California law. You may opt out using the Device controls listed above, which We honour.

Deleting Your data

Because We hold no Personal Data about You on Our servers, there is nothing for Us to delete on request. You can delete any piece from Your gallery within the Application at any time, or remove everything at once by uninstalling the Application.
